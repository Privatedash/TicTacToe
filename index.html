How Infinity Tic-Tac-Toe Works

1

Max 3 Marks Per Player

Each player can only keep at most 3 marks on the board at any time. When you place your 4th mark, your first (oldest) mark dissolves from the board.

2

Expiring Piece Amber Alert ⚠️

When you have 3 marks and it's your turn, your oldest mark pulses with an amber glowing aura and shows badge #1, so you and your opponent know exactly which mark will vanish.

3

Blitz Turn Clocks ⏱️

You have a limited time per turn (configured in Settings). If your clock reaches zero, you forfeit the round to your opponent!

4

Round & Series System 🏆

Play in competitive formats like Best of 3 or Best of 5. The winner of each round earns a point toward claiming the match trophy!
